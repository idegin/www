# About iDegin Technologies

**iDegin Technologies Limited** is an AI transformation and custom software engineering company based in **Abuja, Nigeria**, helping businesses become faster, smarter, and significantly more profitable through intelligent automation, autonomous AI agents, and custom-built digital systems.

We believe technology should not force businesses to change how they work. Instead, technology should adapt to the business.

That's why we begin every engagement by understanding how an organization actually operates—not how it's documented on paper, but how work truly flows across departments, teams, and people. We interview stakeholders and employees, study operational workflows, identify bottlenecks, uncover hidden inefficiencies, and gain a deep understanding of the business before recommending or building any technology.

Only after understanding the business do we design and deploy AI solutions that create measurable impact.

Our mission is simple:

**Help businesses make more money by eliminating operational friction, automating repetitive work, and enabling people to focus on high-value growth activities.**

# Our AI Transformation Methodology

Most organizations purchase AI tools without first understanding where AI will actually create value.

We take a different approach.

Our methodology combines business consulting, systems engineering, process discovery, and AI implementation into a structured transformation journey.

## 1. Business Discovery

Every engagement begins with understanding the business.

We work closely with leadership and employees to learn:

* how work gets done
* how departments collaborate
* current systems and tools
* operational dependencies
* repetitive manual work
* bottlenecks
* approval chains
* reporting processes
* customer journeys
* hidden inefficiencies

Rather than relying solely on documentation, we focus on how the organization actually operates day-to-day.

---

## 2. Process Mapping

After discovery, we map the organization's workflows.

This allows us to visualize:

* business processes
* operational handoffs
* system interactions
* information flow
* productivity gaps
* duplicated work
* automation opportunities

The result is a complete operational picture of the business.
