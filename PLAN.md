## iDegin Technologies Website Information Architecture

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the complete structure, navigation, content hierarchy, and user journey for the iDegin Technologies website.

The website's primary objective is to generate qualified leads by encouraging mid-sized businesses to book an **AI Workflow Audit**.

The website should communicate expertise, trust, measurable business outcomes, and premium consulting—not software outsourcing.

---

# Website Goals

Primary Goal

* Book an AI Workflow Audit

Secondary Goals

* Educate businesses on AI Workforce Transformation
* Build trust and authority
* Showcase methodology
* Generate qualified leads
* Position iDegin as Nigeria's leading AI Workforce Transformation company

# Design Principles

Every page should answer:

1. Why change?
2. Why AI?
3. Why now?
4. Why iDegin?

Every page should end with a clear CTA.

---

# Primary Calls to Action

Book AI Workflow Audit

Schedule Consultation

Contact Us

---

# Content Strategy

Every page should follow this structure:

1. Identify the problem
2. Explain the impact
3. Introduce the solution
4. Explain the methodology
5. Demonstrate outcomes
6. Build trust
7. Encourage action

---
