Ask most AI companies where they begin and they'll tell you: the technology. A model, a framework, a demo. We begin somewhere else entirely—**with your business.**

## Technology-first projects fail quietly

They ship something impressive that nobody adopts. The demo works; the workflow doesn't. Six months later the tool is shelfware and the manual process is back.

The reason is simple: the solution was designed for an assumption, not for how work actually happens on the floor.

## Our order of operations

### 1. Observe the real workflow
We interview the people doing the work, map the steps, and follow the data as it moves between systems. What we document is often different from the official process.

### 2. Find the highest-ROI opportunities
Not everything should be automated. We look for the repetitive, high-volume, rules-based work where AI pays off fastest.

### 3. Design around the operation
We design AI employees that fit your existing tools and hand off gracefully to people—so adoption is natural, not forced.

### 4. Deploy, measure, and optimize
We launch on the workflows that move the numbers, then improve accuracy and coverage over time.

## The principle

> Every recommendation must answer one question: will this measurably improve the business? If the answer is no, we don't build it.

Technology should serve the operation—never the other way around. Start with the business, and the technology has somewhere real to land.
