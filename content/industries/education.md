Education institutions are stretched thin, with staff spending hours on admissions, inquiries, and administration instead of students. As enrollment grows, the bottlenecks grow with it.

An AI workforce takes on the administrative load—processing admissions, supporting students and parents around the clock, and streamlining reporting—so educators can focus on teaching.
