On the floor, manufacturing is precise and fast. In the back office, it's often the opposite—procurement, reporting, and compliance run on spreadsheets, email, and manual re-entry that slow the whole operation down.

An AI workforce closes that gap: raising purchase orders, coordinating with vendors, compiling production reports in real time, and keeping quality and compliance documentation current—so the back office keeps pace with the line.
