Professional services firms sell expertise—but too much of that expertise goes into admin, not clients. When intake, reporting, and follow-up are manual, senior people spend billable hours on work software should handle, and delivery quality drifts as the firm grows.

An AI workforce absorbs the repetitive load: onboarding clients, drafting reports, retrieving precedents, and chasing invoices. Your consultants get their time back, and every engagement runs to the same high standard.
