Healthcare runs on people, but too much of their day goes to scheduling, paperwork, and repetitive inquiries. This playbook outlines how we would design an AI workforce that handles the administrative layer—securely and within your existing controls.

We start with a Workflow Audit across scheduling, intake, records, and patient communication, then deploy AI employees that book and remind, capture intake, organize records, and answer routine questions. Sensitive decisions stay with clinicians; the busywork does not.
