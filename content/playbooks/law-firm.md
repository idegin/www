A law firm sells expertise, but a surprising share of that expertise goes into intake, drafting, and follow-up. This playbook outlines how we would give lawyers their billable hours back.

Following a Workflow Audit across intake, documents, knowledge, and billing, we deploy AI employees that handle onboarding, draft routine documents, surface precedents on demand, and keep billing moving—inside your existing systems, with confidentiality and access controls built in.
