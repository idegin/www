Logistics runs on coordination, and coordination is exactly where manual work piles up. This playbook outlines how we would approach an AI Workforce Transformation for a regional logistics operator—starting, as always, with the business rather than the technology.

We begin with a Workflow Audit: shadowing dispatch, tracking, and customer-support teams to document how work really flows and where hours disappear. From there we design a small team of AI employees that coordinate dispatch, watch for exceptions, and handle status queries—working inside the tools the team already uses. The goal isn't a flashy demo; it's fewer missed SLAs and a calmer operation.
