On the floor, manufacturing is precise and fast. In the back office, it often runs on spreadsheets and re-keyed data. This playbook shows how we would bring the back office up to the speed of the line.

After a Workflow Audit across procurement, production reporting, and quality, we design AI employees that raise purchase orders, keep vendors in sync, compile production reports in real time, and maintain compliance documentation—so supervisors manage exceptions instead of paperwork.
