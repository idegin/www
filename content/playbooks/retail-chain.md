Retail demand is spiky and always-on; staffing isn't. This playbook shows how we would keep service and operations sharp across every store and channel with an AI workforce.

After a Workflow Audit across support, inventory, and order operations, we deploy AI employees that answer customers around the clock, watch stock and trigger reorders, and run order operations end to end—so service holds up even when demand peaks.
